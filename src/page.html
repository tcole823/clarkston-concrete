A plan before the pour

A clear plan.
A confident next step.

Understand the work before it begins, plan around the disruption, and know when your new concrete is ready to use.

  1. 01 / Start with your ideas

    Tell us what you’d like to change.

    Whether you’re replacing worn concrete or creating a new outdoor space, tell us what matters most. Share your location and a rough size to start the conversation.

    You don’t need exact measurements or a finished design to get started.

  2. 02 / Understand the scope

    Know what your estimate covers.

    A useful estimate looks beyond square footage. Talk through your property, the preparation and the finished result so you can make an informed decision.

    Access + drainage
    How equipment will reach the work area and where rainwater needs to go.
    Removal + preparation
    What comes out, how it’s disposed of and what the new concrete will sit on.
    The finished surface
    The layout, thickness, finish and joints, plus any approvals, cleanup or restoration in the scope.
  3. 03 / Know what to expect

    Prepare for the work.
    Enjoy the finished space.

    Before work starts, talk through access, parking, utility locating and timing. Plan for weather and any changes to your daily routine while the area is out of use.

    Once the concrete is placed, give it the time it needs. Get clear guidance on walking, driving, furniture and ongoing care before putting the space to use.

    Looks dry doesn’t mean ready to use. Follow the curing instructions for your project.
