A plan before the pour

From an idea
to everyday use.

A clear project starts with three conversations: what you need, what the site requires, and how to prepare for the work and the days after.

  1. 01 / Your goals

    Start with the space.

    How will you use it, and what needs to change? Share your city or ZIP, a rough size, and any existing concrete.

    A sketch, a few photos or length × width is a useful starting point.

  2. 02 / The site + estimate

    Get the details on paper.

    Talk through the conditions that shape the work, then clarify what the estimate includes.

    Access + drainage
    Equipment access, slopes and places where water collects.
    Removal + preparation
    Existing concrete, disposal, the base and transitions to nearby surfaces.
    The finished surface
    Size, thickness, finish, joints, approvals and cleanup or restoration.
  3. 03 / Preparation + completion

    Plan for the pour.
    Leave time to cure.

    Agree on access, utility locating and timing before work begins. Weather can affect the plan, so arrange a period without use.

    At completion, review the surface and get clear instructions for foot traffic, vehicles and ongoing care.

    Before you use it: follow the project’s curing instructions.
