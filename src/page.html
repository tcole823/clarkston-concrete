From first idea to finished surface

The pour is one part.
The planning is just as important.

Use these three steps to talk through your project, understand the estimate and prepare for the work ahead.

  1. 01

    Share your plans.

    Tell us where the project is, how you’ll use the space and what you want to change. A rough square footage or length and width helps. Mention existing concrete, access limitations and any places where water collects.

    Goals + approximate size
  2. 02

    Work through the details.

    Review site conditions, drainage, removal and disposal, base preparation and the proposed finish. Clarify thickness, joints, approvals and what the estimate includes, including cleanup or restoration around the work area.

    Site conditions + estimate scope
  3. 03

    Prepare. Pour. Give it time.

    Agree on access, utility locating and timing before work starts. Plan for weather and a period without use. At completion, review the surface and get clear instructions for curing, foot traffic, vehicles and ongoing care.

    Preparation + completion + curing
