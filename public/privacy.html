Privacy details

The estimate form collects your name, phone number, optional email, location and project information so Clarkston Concrete can respond to your inquiry.

How requests are handled

Form submissions are emailed through Resend to the business contact responsible for responding. This site does not store submissions in a separate customer database. Email providers may retain messages according to their own settings and policies.

Spam protection

When configured, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies form requests to help prevent automated abuse. It may process browser and network information for that purpose.

Tracking and browser storage

Advertising and analytics tracking are disabled on this review site. The form uses temporary browser session storage to show a confirmation only after a successful request. When production tracking is enabled, a random request ID can prevent repeated conversion events. Names, phone numbers, emails and project details are not included in those events.
