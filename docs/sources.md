## FAQ and project research

Primary contractor and industry sources used to identify homeowner questions and planning considerations:

- Biondo Cement FAQ: https://biondocement.com/frequently-asked-questions/ (estimate scope, curing before use, finish care)
- Cieslak Concrete FAQ: https://cieslakconcrete.com/faq (quote differences, drainage, base and curing)
- Michigan Concrete Association driveways: https://www.miconcrete.org/driveways (drainage, joints, cold-weather considerations and curing)
- Michigan Concrete Association curing: https://www.miconcrete.org/concrete-curing (curing and strength development)
- Michigan Concrete Association care: https://info.miconcrete.org/hubfs/MI-Concrete_ConcreteCare-FullSize_2020-09-25%20%281%29.pdf (ongoing care, loads and drainage)

No competitor prices, warranties, installation guarantees, business claims or specific curing promises are carried over. Site-specific requirements and care instructions must be confirmed for each project. FAQ topics and process copy are general educational guidance, not a representation of Rob's unconfirmed operating procedures.
